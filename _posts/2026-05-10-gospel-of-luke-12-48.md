## Luke 12:48 - From everyone who has been given much, much will be demanded

> "But the one who does not know and does things deserving punishment will be beaten with few blows. From everyone who has been given much, much will be demanded; and from the one who has been entrusted with much, much more will be asked" Gospel of Luke-12:48(NIV).

The quote means that people who have greater advantages, abilities, opportunities, wealth, knowledge, or influence also carry greater responsibility.

If someone has been “given much” — such as education, talent, power, leadership, or resources — society expects them to use those gifts wisely and for the benefit of others, not only for themselves.

For example:

+ A wealthy person may be expected to help others through generosity or ethical leadership.
+ A skilled programmer may be expected to build responsibly and mentor others.
+ A leader may be expected to act with integrity because their decisions affect many people.
